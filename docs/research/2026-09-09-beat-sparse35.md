# Can a complete methodological change beat SPARSE35? A 13-agent design + adversarial review

Produced 2026-09-09 in answer to the user question. Four independent paradigms (image-level DA,
feature-level DA, output representation, ranking objective), each attacked twice. **All eight
verdicts were fatal.** The review also found a normalisation-support bug in this project's own
measurements and pre-registered a prediction for arm 42 that came true.

---

No design survived; all eight verdicts were fatal, and in three of four cases the design's own author priced its modal outcome at or inside the 0.036 VAL macro seed spread before either reviewer touched it. The honest answer to "dá se udělat úplná metodická změna, která by překonala SPARSE35?" is: not by any of these four, and nothing on the table reaches 0.90 — the largest credible gain is roughly +0.02 to +0.05 on the 0.80/0.80 VAL macro (0.672 baseline, 0.920 oracle), and the single highest-value action right now costs CPU-minutes and is not a new method at all.

================================================================
1. THE FINDING THAT SHOULD BE ACTED ON FIRST (measured during this review)
================================================================

The SENSOR-TWIN evidence-checker named a normalisation-support mismatch. I reproduced it on this laptop and extended it, and it is larger and more consequential than stated.

Code fact, verified: training normalises the WHOLE 640x640 render with np.percentile(img,[1,99]) and then crops to 512 (`/Users/michalprusek/PycharmProjects/microtubules/scripts/train_v5.py:419`, gen=640 default at :337); evaluation normalises the WHOLE real frame, which is 1024^2 to 2048^2, with the same function (`/Users/michalprusek/PycharmProjects/microtubules/scripts/eval_v5.py:180` -> norm01 at train_v5.py:637). The 2026-09-07 preprocessing audit verified the function and the percentiles; it did not verify the support.

Measured on all 277 roi303 frames (`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-michalprusek-PycharmProjects-microtubules/43e54bd1-f4c8-4d4e-a7d5-aba4938e3118/scratchpad/support3.py`):
- hp_std of the SAME real pixels: 0.0280 median at full-frame support, 0.1321 median at 640 support. Ratio 1.76x on 1024-1400 frames, 7.50x on 2048 frames.
- Pearson r(log frame area, log normalised hp_std) = -0.649, slope -0.91.
- Per-frame spread q75/q25 = 4.62x; after removing the frame-size trend, 2.54x.

Two consequences, both blocking a run that is already queued:
(a) ARM 42's premise ("the model has been trained at 1.6x the real noise level for the entire project", docs/TODO.md:2505) compares a 640-support render against a full-frame real measurement. At matched support the sign reverses — the render is 2-3x QUIETER than real. The 1.6x is not established by its own instrument.
(b) ARM 42 concluded that the real 4.14x per-frame spread "needs a wider background corpus, not a wider prior — a data question for the user". About 40 % of that log-spread is frame size. The data ask is probably void; do not send the user shopping for backgrounds on the strength of it.

And a third point that cuts deeper than the support question. `build_model` uses norm_op=InstanceNorm2d (train_v5.py:163-164), inference is tiled at 512 with the same tile size as the training crop (predict(), train_v5.py:704-732), and --priors is off by default. conv -> InstanceNorm over matched tile support is exactly invariant to a GLOBAL AFFINE rescale of the input. A percentile-support change is, in the frame interior, exactly such a rescale. So to first order this network cannot see absolute noise amplitude at all — only SNR and texture. That makes hp_std the wrong instrument for an intervention on this trunk, and it makes ARM 42's stated mechanism ill-posed: its actual effect is to raise the render's SNR by ~1.45x, un-matching the per-frame filament-SNR distribution that the NOISE arm's screen had already fitted end to end. Pre-registered prediction, written before the run: ARM 42 lands null-to-negative and trips its own recall falsifier (instance recall below 0.5272).

Caveat I am flagging rather than hiding: the local roi303 copy is an 8-bit export (span_full ~250 ADU), so the exact ratios must be re-measured on the tulen RAW 16-bit block. The code-level mismatch and the InstanceNorm invariance do not depend on bit depth.

2. RANKING (expected headline gain per GPU-hour). Baseline VAL 0.672 at 0.80/0.80/5, oracle 0.920, macro seed spread 0.036, micro spread 0.003.
================================================================

A note on the baseline number: the brief's 0.673 is the "0.80 / Hausdorff-only / 5" row (TP 4614 / FP 2149 / FN 2765); the 0.80/0.80/5 headline row is 0.672, micro 0.645, TP 4558 / FP 2205 / FN 2821 (docs/TODO.md:3279-3284). Several verdicts quoted the two interchangeably. Use 4558/2205/2821 for anatomy arithmetic; micro P 0.674, micro R 0.618.

RANK 0 — SUPPORT / INVARIANCE AUDIT. Not a paradigm; a precondition. Predicted VAL: 0.672, i.e. no gain, and that null IS the result. Cost: CPU-minutes plus one ~10-minute GPU check. Value: it decides whether the queued 6 GPU-h ARM 42 is well-posed, voids a data request to the user, and removes a confound that every future amplitude/noise arm would inherit. Highest value per hour spent, by a wide margin, because the denominator is minutes.

RANK 1 — CURVE-LEVEL ACCEPT ON CACHED MAPS (the salvaged core of SNR-RANK step 4 and MATCHFIELD steps 4-5; survey rows 1+3 fused). Both refuters of both designs converged independently on exactly this residue: kappa-bounded candidate paths seeded from free ends and mask cuts, scored by an integrated evidence statistic with a per-frame noise scale estimated from the frame's own off-mask pixels, accepted paths painted in, existing assembler unchanged. Predicted VAL macro 0.69 modal, band 0.66-0.72. Cost: 2-3 CPU-days, ZERO GPU-hours. Gain per GPU-hour is unbounded because the denominator is zero — that alone puts it first among things that could move the number.

RANK 2 — FEATURE-LEVEL UNSUPERVISED DOMAIN ADAPTATION on the 522-frame unlabelled corpus (marginal alignment of trunk features between renders and real frames; MMD or adversarial). This is the one lane the brief names as open that NONE of the four designs actually built: SENSOR-TWIN and SNR-RANK both collapsed into the NOISE arm / ARM 42 at the image level, and MINRANK explicitly closed the feature lane on a conflation (consistency regularisation does reinforce a wrong ordering; marginal alignment does not assert any ordering on a real pixel). No number, deliberately — there is no measurement in this project supporting one. Gate it at under 1 GPU-hour before predicting anything. Named risk: marginal alignment can drag real faint filaments toward the synthetic background mode, which is ARM 41 at feature level, and 33.5 % of the extractable band on real is off-GT with a large unannotated-filament component.

RANK 3 — OVERLAP-PERMITTING EMISSION (survey row 2 curve queries with per-query masks, or the multiplicity head moved to the emission side rather than into the arc graph). The only route aimed at the failure the record calls the most decision-relevant measurement of the campaign: in domain, on exact labels, junction chimeras are 48.2 % of false positives and 57 % of crossing failures would NOT be fixed by a perfect mask (docs/TODO.md:2170-2195). But on the real benchmark chimeras are 15 % of FPs, so its headline value HERE is small: modal first build below 0.50 per the survey's own assessment, converged optimistic 0.70-0.75. Cost ~2 weeks build plus ~1 GPU-week for seeds and sweeps. Worst gain per GPU-hour, highest long-run ceiling, and the only route with any path above 0.75.

DEAD, do not build: SENSOR-TWIN as written; MINRANK in both its stop-grad and non-stop-grad forms; SNR-RANK steps 1/2/3/5; MATCHFIELD's four-channel head plus generator emission.

Nothing above, alone or combined, reaches 0.90. The honest aggregate ceiling of everything on this list is ~0.72-0.75 on VAL against a measured oracle of 0.920.

3. BUILD ORDER FOR RANK 1, first step cheap and falsifiable
================================================================

STEP 0 (CPU-minutes, do it today, it also serves RANK 0). On tulen RAW: re-run `diag_background_richness.py` at matched support and re-measure the frame-size trend; then the InstanceNorm check — feed one real frame to the shipped SPARSE35 checkpoint normalised two ways (full-frame percentiles vs a size-matched 640 window applied globally) and diff the two probability maps. KILL LINE: if max |dp| < 0.01 over the frame, the network is amplitude-invariant as predicted, the support mismatch is inert for the model, and ARM 42's amplitude mechanism is void — reclassify ARM 42 as an SNR intervention and re-declare or cancel it. If max |dp| > 0.05, the mismatch is live and a support-matched inference sweep goes to the front of the queue.

STEP 1, THE FALSIFIER (~1 CPU-day, 0 GPU-h, cached SPARSE35 native VAL maps, no new training, no new head). Build the candidate set: ridge points / free ends / mask cuts from the cached map, kappa-bounded linking at the derived 0.25 rad/px, integrated evidence score on the raw frame with a per-frame robust noise scale from the frame's own off-mask pixels. Read TWO things, paired per frame over the 139 VAL frames:
  (a) GENERATION BOUND (survey row 3's own gate): of the ~2678 addressable GT curves, how many acquire a completing candidate. KILL LINE: under half, the direction dies for free.
  (b) END-TO-END Delta macro at 0.80/0.80 AND at 0.80 + end_tol 25, against a PLAIN THRESHOLD DROP MATCHED ON ADDED FOREGROUND. That control is mandatory and is missing from both original designs — it is the exact control that closed overlap-conditioned filling. KILL LINE: Delta macro <= +0.036 (the seed spread), or the foreground-matched threshold drop equals or beats it, or off_other / overshoot / covered_split rise in proportion to TP (the NOISE signature: +141 FP for +24 TP).
Report micro F1 (spread 0.003) as the resolving statistic, not macro, and report the 32 near-empty frames as a separate pre-declared row — they carry 23 % of the macro and are where class-B unannotated-filament false positives cost most.

STEP 2, only if step 1 passes both gates. Declare an explicit stop rule before the run (terminate after k consecutive steps below the frame's noise floor, k fixed) and read the primary at end_tol 0 and 25 side by side, because overshoot q50 is already 17 px and end placement alone is worth 0.113 macro. Pass accepted curves to the assembler BOTH ways — rasterised (the confound-free control) and as arcs (the treatment) — rather than crippling the treatment to avoid the confound.

STEP 3. Junction identity (n=2243) and the covered_split CHIMERA count are read alongside F1 in every arm, with a pre-declared kill if identity falls regardless of F1. Painting kappa-bounded paths into a binary mask where two filaments sit 3 px apart is a merge mechanism, and the macro will hide it.

4. CLOSED ROUTES IN DISGUISE
================================================================

- SENSOR-TWIN components A and C are the NOISE arm's parameterisation (calib_V6_noise.json already used an absolute reference with a per-frame log-uniform photon scale and matched the real filament-SNR quantiles), and its amplitude lever is ARM 42, declared 03:05 today and queued. Component B(ii), row/column banding, is measured ABSENT: real acf_row 0.2039 vs acf_col 0.2044. Component B(i), a heavy read-noise tail fitted to kurtosis 3.71, targets a statistic that is spatially heterogeneous CONTENT, not a stationary sensor field. Its own primary citation (READ 39) concludes "the missing ingredient is elongated non-filament structure, not texture statistics", and the design then forbids itself from touching ridge_share.
- MINRANK with stop-grad is a per-image hard-mined pos_weight: the reference r does not exist at inference, so what ships is a global confidence rescale, whose entire measured value is the threshold sweep (+0.014 micro recall for 23 % more foreground). Without stop-grad it is ARM 41 with a learned negative set. Its L_lat term is 0.0-THIN in a new coat (+0.071 at a shared threshold, +0.0095 once thresholds were re-swept per model).
- SNR-RANK steps 1-3 are the NOISE arm mirrored (whitening instead of colouring) plus ARM 42; step 5's "real empty-field null" is calibrated on the 428 crops that ARE the generator's compositing backgrounds (train_v5.py:920, docs/protocol.md:17) — the source domain, not the target. Its G1 gate cannot fail, because SPARSE35 was trained with those exact pixels labelled background.
- MATCHFIELD's front end (ridge points by NMS on a regression field, gated at a low existence floor) is the GP/GU heat-ridge screen already run on the cached HEAT dump: identity rose monotonically 0.411 -> 0.551 while macro F1 fell monotonically, -0.011 to -0.031. Its accept/reject step is genuinely outside the 11-variant decoder family; its front half is not.

================================================================
5. FATAL OBJECTIONS I AM ACCEPTING RATHER THAN REPAIRING, AND WHAT THAT COSTS
================================================================

- Class B is unfilterable. A measurable share of off-GT band mass is real filaments the annotator did not draw; they are curve-coherent, so every candidate-growth or curve-integration route AMPLIFIES them relative to a per-pixel rule, and suppressing them means fitting the annotation convention, which is forbidden. Cost of accepting: caps RANK 1 at ~0.72 and is the single most likely way it lands null.
- 13 % of GT is never found at any threshold, capping recall at 0.87 before any decoder runs. That is generator contrast realism. Cost: nothing in the ranking touches it; it is a permanent ~0.05-0.08 charge against the 0.920 ceiling.
- The in-domain crossing failure (48.2 % chimeras, 57 % not fixable by a perfect mask) is untouched by RANK 0, 1 and 2. Cost: those routes cannot exceed ~0.75 no matter how well they work, and only RANK 3 addresses it.
- Precision is nearly unmovable by any anti-hallucination mechanism: 88 % of FPs are fragments and chimeras of ANNOTATED filaments, 6 % visible unannotated ridge, 6 % nothing visible. Removing every truly hallucinated FP is worth about +0.012 at the headline convention. Cost: the entire prize must come from recall, and every measured recall gain in this project has been paid for at a net loss.

6. WHAT THE USER MUST DECIDE OR SUPPLY
================================================================

1. ARM 42: hold it. Section 1 says its premise is measured across mismatched supports and its mechanism is likely inert under InstanceNorm. A 10-minute check decides it before 6 GPU-hours are spent. This is a decision, not a suggestion, because the arm is queued.
2. The background-corpus request from ARM 42 ("matching the real 4.14x spread needs a wider background corpus") should be withdrawn pending the RAW re-measure — roughly 40 % of that spread is frame size.
3. A POLICY RULING on feature-level marginal alignment (MMD / adversarial) using the 522-frame unlabelled corpus. It creates no label on any real image and asserts no ordering, so it is not pseudo-labelling under the standing rule — but it is the one lane nobody has ruled on, and RANK 2 cannot start without the ruling.
4. ANNOTATION TIME, small and specific: one annotator, ~10 VAL frames, drawing EVERY visible filament rather than the usual selection. Not to train on and not to fit the convention — to measure the class-B tax, which is currently estimated from a biased sample of 30 predictions and is the load-bearing unknown in every recall-side prediction above.
5. A DECISION ON THE TARGET. Under the headline convention the model is 0.672 and the oracle through the same assembler is 0.920; the decomposition is annotation/convention ~0.19, assembler ~0.00, map fidelity ~0.26. Reaching 0.90 means 98 % of a perfect mask. Nothing surveyed, designed, or refuted in this exercise has a credible path there. If 0.90 is to remain the target, the honest plan is RANK 3 plus generator contrast realism over months, not a decoder or an objective swapped in weeks.
