# Where to spend the next month: a paradigm survey read against this project's own measurements

## 0. The bottleneck restated in one paragraph

Neither the assembler nor the data is the problem. Instancer A on an oracle mask scores 0.8764 on roi303 VAL native (`docs/TODO.md:158`), 0.951 tuned (method audit §1, 0.0-MASK), against 0.500 to 0.520 on the model's mask. In-domain on exact-label synthetic frames (`data/synth_eval_s35`, 48 frames, `docs/TODO.md:1574`) it also scores 0.4999, coincidentally the same figure as the real VAL 1.5x row at `docs/TODO.md:171`; the two are different blocks. Real VAL and in-domain synth thus coincide, and the ~0.03 gap exists only against TEST (0.472 +/- 0.052, 137 frames, per the brief). What fails is the map's *value* on weak stretches: 84 % of failing crossings have an arm already below 0.98 at 8 to 20 px from the junction, 45 % have an arm no instance reaches (`docs/TODO.md:235`), 97.6 % have a disconnected mask and 62.1 % an arm missing outright (`docs/research/2026-09-04-junctions-and-crossings.md` §0). The linker is exonerated: of 1286 mask cuts, 67 % are bridged by the arc graph and 24 % by the free-end linker, `B_EVIDENCE` firing 0 times (`docs/TODO.md:1198-1203`). The gap is confidence on arms that exist in the image and not in the mask.

**Prerequisite every row inherits, and nobody has run it.** Decompose the in-domain 0.4999 into failure classes (crossings, 2 to 6 px parallels, endpoint overshoot, mid-filament breaks) on exact synthetic GT: two CPU-hours. If that loss is endpoint bookkeeping rather than ridge fidelity, rows 2 and 4 lose their premise before any build.

## 2. The top three in detail

### 2.1 Matched-filter free-end growth

**Mechanism.** Extend each free end in ~2 px steps under an oriented matched filter on the raw frame, polarity-agnostic (absolute response, because IRM flips dark to bright), normalised by a local noise estimate, stopping at the noise floor; hand the extended ends to the existing gap linker. **How it differs from what failed.** The reviewer framed this as swapping `bridge_evidence(prob, a, b)` at `src/instance/instancer_a.py:200-202` for a raw-image score. **That version is closed on arrival:** `B_EVIDENCE` fired 0 of 1286 cuts, and the record states `bridge_thr`, `link_max_gap`, `gap_floor` and `c_open_link` are not levers, at 0 to 4 % of cuts each (`docs/TODO.md:1198-1203`); a gate that rejects nothing can only reject more. Growth differs in kind: it creates a free-end partner where none exists, the only route to the 45 % of crossings no instance reaches. Nor is it the NOISE arm, which changed the training distribution and landed null because its recovery was diffuse; growth targets arms of *annotated* crossings.

**Published evidence.** No evidence found at 3.5 px FWHM or SNR 0.4 to 2; the parent literature (marked point processes for line networks) has no verified runtime in the sources consulted, and its data term was built for high-contrast roads at metre scale, sign-ambiguous under polarity flips. The argument is arithmetic, not borrowed.

**Pre-registered experiment.** Cached SPARSE35 native VAL maps, shipped threshold, nothing else changed, paired per frame so seed noise does not apply. Read junction identity (n = 2043, resolvable), primary macro, macro >= 10 GT, micro, plus `off_other` and overshoot counts. **Success:** identity AND primary macro both rise. **Falsifier:** identity flat, meaning the arms are not recoverable from the raw image either, which closes the analysis-by-synthesis family; or identity up with macro flat while `off_other` and overshoot rise in proportion to TP, which is NOISE repeating.

### 2.2 TopoBDA

**Mechanism.** Deformable attention whose per-head reference points are the query's own predicted Bezier control points, refined each decoder layer, with one-to-many auxiliary supervision and a per-query mask head: the query pools along its own curve hypothesis, not at fixed grid locations.

**Published evidence, with object width stated.** On thin polyline centrelines in BEV road-graph mapping (metre-scale road lanes, not filaments), curve-anchored deformable attention beats masked attention 40.8 vs 35.8 DET_l and 48.0 vs 42.6 OLS_l at a quarter of the decoder cost (10.0 vs 41.6 GFLOPs); the sweep supplied no arXiv ID. No evidence found at 3.5 px FWHM, none at SNR 0.4 to 2, none on endpoint placement (OLS does not measure ends); results are single-run by the authors' admission.

**How it differs from the 0.0128 set-prediction failure.** The post-mortem named four absent ingredients: attention anchored on the query's own geometry rather than stride-8 global memory (position quantised at 8 px against a 5 px tolerance), per-layer iterative refinement rather than one emission from the final hidden state, one-to-many auxiliary supervision, and a per-query mask head. TopoBDA has all four, so the prior result is evidence against one under-built configuration, not against the paradigm. To build: a curve-query decoder on the existing trunk, Bezier targets from the generator's exact polylines including the amodal continuation through a crossing (free on synth, impossible on real), three seeds minimum.

**Pre-registered experiment (do not build first).** Probe the load-bearing claim, that pooling along a curve hypothesis extracts evidence a per-pixel head cannot. Confound controlled: pool *only* the sub-threshold stretch, since pooling a whole arm separates filament from background trivially via the strong parts. On the failed VAL crossings, isolate the 8 to 20 px run where p < 0.98, pool frozen features along it conditioned on the tangent, negatives being same-length same-tangent stretches displaced 5 to 8 px laterally; train a small MLP. **Success:** pooled AUC beats the per-pixel map's AUC on those pixels by a resolvable margin. **Falsifier:** it does not, and the family dies for one GPU-hour. If it passes, the build gate is the standing one (beat instancer A's 0.4166 on 40 fresh synthetic frames), then real VAL reporting primary and end_tol 25 side by side.

### 2.3 A* candidate-path graph with a learned path classifier

**Mechanism.** Nodes at epsilon spacing from a low-threshold skeleton plus a grid; edges are A* shortest paths with per-pixel cost 1.1-p; a second head, jointly trained with segmentation, takes the raw image plus a candidate-path mask and emits P(real); global selection replaces the walk. **Why it reaches a pool nothing else does, stated against itself.** `linker.py` scores pairs of `ArmEnd`s that already exist in the arc graph built from the thresholded mask, so it cannot invent a candidate for a sub-threshold arm. Geodesics can, and the failing arms are not at zero probability (p_far_min p50 0.29): 973 unmatched plus 1705 coverage-only GT curves are addressable. But the closed linker null is not weak evidence: `linker.py` already won on candidate ranking with hand features (precision@k 0.862 vs 0.776 for the geometric cost on held-out synthetic) and produced *identical* end-to-end true positives (229) on real VAL, so better ranking over the same candidate set converts to zero. The difference is entirely in candidate *generation*, which is why gate 1 measures generation, not scoring. Mosinska, Kozinski and Fua report joint segmentation plus path classification on roads and 2D neurites; the sweep supplied no numeric ablation and no arXiv ID, so none is quoted. No evidence found at 3.5 px FWHM or SNR 0.4 to 2.

**Pre-registered experiment.** (1) Build the candidate graph on cached maps, label against GT at >90 % overlap, 4 to 8 CPU-hours. (2) Before training anything, read the generation bound. **Falsifier:** fewer than half of the 2678 addressable GT curves acquire a completing candidate, and the direction dies for free. (3) Only then train the path head on synthetic candidates from exact centerlines, frozen trunk plus small head, ~1 GPU-day. **Falsifier:** ROC-AUC < 0.80 on the split that matters, real faint arm versus shortcut between two filaments or a hop between 2 to 6 px parallels. The heat-ridge screen found the coupling that would sink it: at heat 0.5 to 0.7 the identity gain and the spurs are the same pixels, so under a 95 % precision rule the accept threshold must sit where FP = 0.

## 3. What the sweep ruled out, and why

**Autoregressive tracing (4).** *Pick and Trace:* tip detector is a single point of failure under one-to-one matching; authors scope out crossings and bundles. *DeepBranchTracer* (2402.01187): worst Jump Error of every method compared on three of five datasets (retinal vessels, aerial roads), taking the next node from a probability maximum with no connectivity cue, which is the close-parallel failure. *Trexplorer Super:* duplicate suppression removes one member of every bundle; ~2M iterations on 4x A100 is far outside budget. *Wolterink* (1810.03143): step length is the local radius, so it decides inside the dip rather than spanning it.

**Query-and-curve (7).** *CLRerNet/LaneIoU:* a soft surrogate has no gradient near a hard 0.95 step, and divides by dy. *CLRNet:* strictest column is IoU >= 0.90 on 30 px wide lane masks, 8.5x wider than a 3.5 px filament. *BezierLaneNet:* overfits the training curvature prior (-20.30 F1 without strong augmentation); this generator's curvature is deliberately unfitted. *ADNet:* a Gaussian heat head on this backbone already scored -0.030 native. *Polar R-CNN:* assembly-side duplicate removal, ~+0.003 scaled here. *LaneGAP:* junction evidence is road-graph merges and forks. *Mask2Former/MaskDINO:* loses to curve-anchored attention on thin polylines at 4x decoder cost.

**Analysis-by-synthesis (5).** *DECODE, deltaMic:* fitting the forward model to real data caps at 0.50, the domain gap being ~0.03. *Deep Vectorization, Bezier Splatting:* both terminate curves on pixel evidence, so overshoot rises in a known direction. *BLISS:* not an experiment, but one cheap untried idea for the per-frame calibration defect, per-image PSF and background as network inputs.

**Topology and generative (5).** *Supervoxel critical-component loss, Topograph:* aimed at merges the benchmark cannot resolve (266 close-parallel pairs, median 0 per frame; -0.031 is 1.0 SE). *Cake-wavelet orientation lifts, diffusion amodal shape priors:* unbuilt machinery, no thin-filament number in regime. *CurvSegFlow:* refuted as a method, but its dataset (237 real in-vitro interference-microscopy microtubule frames with manual traces, apparently from the collaborating lab) is a real asset and a mandatory citation.

**Graph and connectivity (6).** *Hierarchical GNN:* builds at p > 0.5 on a ~1.00 coverage claim that is a median artefact; loss-carrying frames have 30 to 34 % of GT under p < 0.5. *Lifted multicut:* node set comes from the thresholded mask, so the 45 % missing-arm pool has no node; `w_ori` measures exactly 0.000 on predicted masks, twice. *PatchPerPix:* highest false-merge count of its baselines in third-party measurement, and the cheap version of its fix (a thin target) was null-to-negative in all four factorial cells. *Flood-filling networks:* S = 0.11 to 0.14 at the strictest thin-filament thresholds, seeds inherited from the same map. *Sat2Graph* (2007.09547): fixes crossings without topological junctions, not our failure. *FlyTracing / GALA learned agglomeration:* `linker.py` already won on ranking (precision@k 0.862 vs 0.776) and converted to identical 229 TP, so a richer scorer over a 91 %-solved candidate set has zero conversion.

**Foundation and real supervision (6 refuted, plus UCS demoted).** *nnPU, ignore-region loss, annotator-preference head, VessShape dose-response, background recalibration:* each repairs an intervention (adding real data) measured to cost 0.043 to 0.075, and each burns the 141-frame tuning block or the 140-frame TEST half. *Chiu mixed-vs-finetune:* its own remedy is a qualitative rubric, because Dice disagrees with usable output under incomplete labels, and this metric charges over-extension at full price. *UCS:* SAM's ViT is stride-16 against a 5 px tolerance and 3.5 px filaments; its entire gain (F1 33.07 to 77.74, on real-trained vessels much thicker than 3.5 px) is spent recovering detail nnU-Net's stride-1 skips never discard; its worst degradation is under realistic correlated noise, this project's confirmed domain. Its Gate A is promoted above the table.

## 4. The honest answer on 0.90

**Under the strict rule (0.95/0.95, tol 5, one-to-one), 0.90 is not reachable and no surveyed paradigm claims it.** An oracle mask through instancer A scores 0.8764 on VAL native and 0.951 only after instancer tuning (`docs/TODO.md:158`), so a *perfect* map barely clears the target, and only with a tuned assembler. Below that sit three charges the model does not own: 13 % of GT is never found at any threshold (`docs/research/2026-09-04-thin-filament-continuity.md` §0), capping recall at 0.87 before any decoder runs, which is generator contrast realism rather than architecture; 0.113 macro and 943 TP is pure end placement, recovered by end_tol 25 on identical predictions (`docs/TODO.md:899-902`); and ~17 % of false positives are overshoot and gap-bridge, also annotation-owned. The highest ceiling any survivor computed is 0.70 (TopoBDA's best case at detection parity with zero new failure modes); the realistic band across all five rows is 0.50 to 0.65.

**Under the 0.80/0.80 convention the field commonly uses the picture inverts: on roi303 VAL the model is already at 0.672, and 0.704 with end_tol 25** (`docs/TODO.md:901-902`), against the same block's strict 0.527. **No measurement found** for an oracle mask under 0.80/0.80, so whether 0.90 is reachable there cannot be stated from the record; measure it, at the cost of one re-score of cached predictions. Two caveats. The convention change is worth +0.145 on identical predictions, so any cross-paper comparison that does not state its rule is uninterpretable. And MT-34's 585 half is 69 % model-drawn against a measured agreement bias of +0.20 to +0.28 on model-drawn versus human-drawn GT, so 0.90 on MT-34 alone would be a statement about annotator agreement, not a physical claim.
